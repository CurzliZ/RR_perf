The PRR Formula & Shrinkage Architecture

PRR = 50 + 10 × z
A • Impact (50% Weight)
∑ WP_add / (balls + 100)

Empirical Bayesian shrinkage pulling small hot streaks down towards league mean. High-volume performers retain pure impact.

B • Reliability (30% Weight)
Hit Rate shrunk by 8 matches

Share of matches (≥4 bat / 6 bowl balls) with positive net win probability added. Protects against single-game wonders.

C • vs Own Baseline (20% Weight)
WP_add/lev(Press) − WP_add/lev(Norm)

Measures whether a player gears up or wilts compared to their own ordinary performance, shrunk equivalently.

Model Robustness, Diagnostic Checks & Reproducibility

Rigorous statistical stress tests, split-half correlations, known data limitations, and executable pipeline specification.

Check A: Bootstrap Resampling
300 Resamples

Resampling by match produced the 90% confidence intervals and rank intervals. Captures match clustering effects and avoids overstating certainty.

Check B: Threshold Sensitivity
Spearman ρ ≥ 0.87

Moving cutoff to top 30% or top 10% kept bowler ranks exceptionally stable (ρ = 0.99 and 0.87) and batter ranks stable (ρ = 0.84 and 0.71).

Check C: Split-Half Reliability
ρ = 0.20 & 0.26

Odd vs even match ID split showed raw ρ = 0.11 (bat) & 0.15 (bowl), correcting to 0.20 and 0.26. Confirms extreme noise in raw small samples — shrinkage is indispensable!

Explicit Model Limitations & Boundary Conditions

  • • Extras Inferred: Wides/no-balls = 1 run; byes/leg-byes = 1 run each. Final match scores within ~3 runs of true score.
  • • 1st Innings Model Weakness: AUC 0.67 ignores pitch conditions and team strength asymmetries.
  • • No Opposition Quality Adjustment: Deliveries against elite pacers vs part-timers are treated equally in raw state.
  • • Unweighted Formats: IPL and T20Is pooled together without competition-strength weight damping.
  • • Run-Out Attribution: Run-outs are credited directly to striker/bowler rather than fielders.
  • • Off-Field Exclusions: Age, fitness, auction availability, and international calendar clashes are outside model scope.
